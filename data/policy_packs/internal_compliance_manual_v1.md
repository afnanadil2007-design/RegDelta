# Internal Compliance Manual — Meridian Broking Private Limited

> **⚠️ SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Meridian Broking Private Limited does not exist.
> This manual was written for the RegDelta demonstration. It imitates the
> structure and specificity of a real mid-sized Indian broker's compliance
> manual so that SEBI circulars can genuinely match, modify, or conflict with
> its clauses.
>
> This is **not legal advice** and must not be relied on as a compliance
> document. The clause numbers, timelines, and thresholds below were invented
> to be plausible, not to be correct.

Version 1.0 · Owner: Head of Compliance · Applies to: all business units

---

## Part A — Client Onboarding and KYC

### A.1 Client due diligence before activation
No trading account shall be activated until client due diligence is complete
and recorded in the onboarding system. The dealing desk is prohibited from
accepting orders on an account in `PENDING_KYC` status.

### A.2 Identity verification
The onboarding team shall verify the client's Permanent Account Number against
the income tax database and retain the verification response with the client
file. A manual override requires written approval from the Head of Compliance.

### A.3 In-person verification
In-person verification, whether physical or video-based, shall be completed
within **fifteen (15) calendar days** of account opening. Accounts not verified
within this window shall be suspended from trading.

### A.4 Proof of address
A self-attested proof of address shall be obtained at onboarding. Where the
correspondence address differs from the permanent address, both shall be
recorded.

### A.5 Periodic re-KYC
Re-KYC shall be carried out every **ten (10) years** for clients categorised as
low risk, every eight years for medium risk, and every two years for high risk.

### A.6 Risk categorisation
Every client shall be assigned a risk category at onboarding based on the
client risk-scoring matrix maintained by the Compliance function, and the
category shall be reviewed at each re-KYC.

### A.7 Beneficial ownership
For non-individual clients, beneficial owners holding more than 10% shall be
identified and their identity documents retained.

### A.8 Rejected applications
Records of rejected account applications, including the reason for rejection,
shall be retained for five years.

## Part B — Order Handling and Execution

### B.1 Order time-stamping
Every client order shall be time-stamped at the point of receipt, whether
received by telephone, in person, or electronically. The order management
system shall reject any order lacking a receipt timestamp.

### B.2 Order audit trail
An audit trail of every order modification and cancellation shall be maintained
for **five (5) years** and shall be retrievable by client code and by trading
day.

### B.3 Execution sequence
Client orders shall be executed in the sequence in which they are received,
unless the client has given a contrary instruction recorded in writing.

### B.4 Proprietary order segregation
Client orders shall not be aggregated with proprietary orders. Proprietary
trading shall be conducted through a separately identified terminal.

### B.5 Telephone recording
All telephone lines used by the dealing desk shall be recorded, and recordings
shall be preserved for **six (6) months**.

### B.6 Error account
Trades booked to the error account shall be reviewed and cleared by the
Compliance function within two trading days.

### B.7 Algorithmic order approval
No algorithmic order-routing strategy shall be deployed to production without
written approval from the Head of Risk and exchange approval where required.

## Part C — Margin Collection and Settlement

### C.1 Upfront margin
Upfront margin shall be collected from clients before the acceptance of orders
in both the cash and derivatives segments. The risk management system shall
block orders where the available margin is insufficient.

### C.2 Acceptable collateral
Margin may be collected as cash, cash equivalents, or securities from the
approved collateral list maintained by the Head of Risk, subject to the
prescribed haircuts.

### C.3 Short-collection reporting
Instances of short-collection of margin shall be reported to the Stock Exchange
by **T+2 days**.

### C.4 Penalty tracking
Penalties levied for short collection shall be recorded against the responsible
business unit and reported monthly to the Risk Committee.

### C.5 Evidence retention
Evidence of margin collection, including collateral valuation records, shall be
retained for **five (5) years**.

### C.6 Client funds segregation
Client funds shall be held in a designated client bank account and shall not be
used to meet proprietary obligations under any circumstance.

### C.7 Settlement of client payouts
Client payouts shall be released within one working day of settlement, except
where a lien has been recorded and communicated to the client.

## Part D — Risk Disclosure

### D.1 Risk disclosure document
A risk disclosure document shall be furnished to every client before the first
trade is executed, and the client's acknowledgement shall be recorded.

### D.2 Electronic acknowledgement
Electronic acknowledgement of the risk disclosure document is acceptable where
it is captured with a timestamp and an audit trail.

### D.3 Display on trading interface
The prescribed risk disclosures shall be displayed prominently on all
client-facing trading interfaces, including the mobile application.

### D.4 Periodic review
Risk disclosure content shall be reviewed at least once every **three (3)
years**, and immediately upon a change in regulatory requirement.

### D.5 Derivatives risk warning
Clients trading in derivatives shall additionally be shown the prescribed
warning regarding the proportion of loss-making individual traders.

## Part E — Investor Grievance Redressal

### E.1 Resolution timeline
Investor complaints shall be resolved within **thirty (30) calendar days** of
receipt.

### E.2 Designated officer
A compliance officer shall be designated as responsible for grievance
redressal, and the officer's name and contact details shall be published on the
firm's website.

### E.3 Complaint register
A register of complaints shall be maintained recording receipt date, nature,
resolution date, and outcome.

### E.4 Exchange reporting
A monthly statement of pending complaints shall be submitted to the Stock
Exchange within seven days of month end.

### E.5 SCORES registration
The firm shall remain registered on the SCORES platform and shall respond to
complaints routed through it within the timelines specified by the platform.

## Part F — Record Retention

### F.1 Minimum retention period
Books of account, records, and documents shall be preserved for a minimum of
**five (5) years**.

### F.2 Records under investigation
Where a matter is under investigation or subject to a pending proceeding,
related records shall be preserved until the proceeding concludes,
notwithstanding clause F.1.

### F.3 Electronic form
Electronic records shall be maintained in a tamper-evident, readily retrievable
form, with integrity checks performed annually.

### F.4 Production during inspection
Records shall be produced during inspection within **five (5) working days** of
a written request.

### F.5 Disposal
Records shall be disposed of only after the retention period expires and only
with written authorisation from the Head of Compliance.

## Part G — Cyber Security

### G.1 Cyber security policy
A documented cyber security policy shall be maintained and approved by the
Board at least annually.

### G.2 Incident reporting
Cyber security incidents shall be reported to SEBI and to the relevant Stock
Exchange within **twenty-four (24) hours** of detection.

### G.3 Vulnerability assessment
A Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Test shall be conducted at least
once every financial year by an external assessor.

### G.4 Chief Information Security Officer
A Chief Information Security Officer shall be appointed and shall report
functionally to the Board's Risk Committee.

### G.5 Disaster recovery
Critical systems shall be subjected to a disaster recovery drill every **twelve
(12) months**, and the results shall be reported to the Risk Committee.

### G.6 Access review
Privileged access to trading and settlement systems shall be reviewed
quarterly, and dormant accounts shall be disabled.

### G.7 Third-party systems
Vendors with access to client data shall be subject to an annual security
review and a contractual obligation to report incidents.

## Part H — Reporting and Governance

### H.1 Internal audit
A half-yearly internal audit report shall be submitted to the Stock Exchange
within the prescribed timeline.

### H.2 Compliance certificate
The quarterly compliance certificate shall be filed within **twenty-one (21)
days** of the end of each quarter.

### H.3 Change in control
Any change in control, or in the shareholding of the firm exceeding the
prescribed threshold, shall be intimated to SEBI within **thirty (30) days**.

### H.4 System audit
An annual system audit shall be conducted by a CERT-In empanelled auditor and
the report placed before the Board.

### H.5 Compliance training
All client-facing staff shall complete compliance training annually, and
attendance records shall be retained for three years.

### H.6 Board reporting
The Head of Compliance shall report to the Board at least quarterly on
regulatory changes, open findings, and remediation status.
